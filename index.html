Cash Counter Pro - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Introduction

Cash Counter Pro is a cash counting and calculation application. This policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

Information and Storage

The app stores shop names, denomination quantities, cash totals and saved entries locally on your device to provide its features. This information is not sent to our own servers by the app's local storage functionality.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, usage information and other data as described in Google's policies.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Sharing

Reports are shared only when you choose to use the app's share feature. Information handled by advertising services is subject to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

This app is not specifically directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an advertising service, please contact us.

Security and Changes

We take reasonable steps to explain the app's data handling. This policy may be updated when the app's features or practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the developer contact details provided on Google Play.
